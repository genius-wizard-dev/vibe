---
name: research.discuss
description: Resolve ambiguous items through focused discussion and decision logging.
---

Read:

- `.vibe/research/<research>/analysis.md`
- unresolved risks and unknowns

For each unresolved item:

- ask one focused question
- propose up to 2 options
- record selected decision and rationale

Write to:

- `.vibe/research/<research>/discussion.md`
- `.vibe/research/<research>/decisions.md`

Set `discuss: done` when no critical ambiguity remains.
