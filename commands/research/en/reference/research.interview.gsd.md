# research.setup Interview Guide (GSD)

Use this guide in `/research.setup` Step 2.

## GSD Framing

Before deep analysis, force these clarifications:

1. Output: what must be delivered after this research?
2. Done criteria: how will we know research is usable?
3. Scope: what is in and out?
4. Blockers: what information is still missing?
5. First move: what should design do next?

## Interview Questions

1. System goal and business objective
2. Primary and secondary user groups
3. Main user journeys
4. Frontend stack preference
5. Backend stack preference
6. Data and storage preference
7. Third-party integrations
8. Security, privacy, and compliance constraints
9. Reliability, scale, and latency expectations
10. Hard boundaries and non-goals

## Follow-up Prompts

- "What would make this fail in production?"
- "What decision has the highest cost if wrong?"
- "What part is still ambiguous and needs design spikes?"
